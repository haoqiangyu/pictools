App Name
Pictools - Image Tools

Subtitle
On-device image tools

Promotional Text
Handle everyday image tasks directly on your device, without uploading your images.

Description
Pictools is a lightweight image toolbox built with privacy in mind. No account is required: choose an image and start working.

Key features:
- Image comparison: inspect differences with slider, side-by-side, and overlay views.
- Resize and crop: enter exact dimensions, lock the aspect ratio, or crop to a common ratio.
- Brightness enhancement: improve exposure and shadow detail, then compare the result with the original.
- Format conversion: batch convert PNG, JPG, WebP, BMP, ICO, and TIFF images.
- Flexible export: choose images and export locations through the iOS system file picker.

Privacy first:
Pictools requires no sign-in and includes no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. Image processing takes place on your device, and the app does not upload your images to us. Choose a display language in Settings or follow the system language.

What's New
First iOS release with image comparison, resizing, aspect-ratio cropping, brightness enhancement, and format conversion. All image processing runs on your device.
